Files are opened and processed inside your browser on your own device, and they are not uploaded to a PDF4Me server. Some tools download their processing engine (WebAssembly) the first time you use them. The signing and timestamp tools may contact a certificate authority or timestamp server; the document itself is not sent.

Any device with an up-to-date browser: Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS or Android. Everything runs on your own device's memory, so very large files can be slow on an older phone.

Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a server, so the content of your papers does not pass through our systems. Still, use your own device and close the tab when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

The standard tools do not send files to a server, so we hold no copy of them. A few preferences, such as language and keyboard shortcuts, are stored in your own browser, and you can clear them any time by clearing the site's data.

If your papers are photos or scans, turn them into PDFs with the image to PDF tools first. Then open Merge PDF, add the files, drag them into the right order and download the combined file.
